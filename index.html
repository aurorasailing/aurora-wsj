Aurora Sailing · WSJ2027 Wind. Live wind for the river-sailing at the 26th World Scout Jamboree (Poland 2027), Marina Błotnik. Automatic observations from IMGW-PIB (the Polish met service — Świbno, Gdynia, Hel and Gdańsk airport), a modelled reading at the marina point from Open-Meteo, and the on-water anemometer at Górki Zachodnie (NCŻ) via Holfuy station #1441. Reading the numbers:
  • Refresh — the IMGW coastal stations report about hourly, and Open-Meteo's marina reading updates on a similar cadence, so "Updated N min ago" cycling up to ~an hour is normal.
  • Gust — the strongest gust in the latest reading, where the source carries one (the marina and Górki Zachodnie do; the hourly IMGW synop stations don't).
  • Trend — building or easing over the last couple of hours, shown where a station carries enough history (the marina point does).
  • F5 fresh breeze — the Beaufort force and its name, a 0–12 scale for wind strength.
  • Górki Zachodnie (NCŻ) — the on-water anemometer at the sailing centre, read live from the public Holfuy #1441 widget; a current reading with no 24-hour history.
Observations are automatic and not quality-checked — always confirm conditions on the water before you sail.
