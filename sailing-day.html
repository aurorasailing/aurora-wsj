What the pictures mean

  • 🚣Too calm under 5 kt — not much wind, grab the oars.
  • ⛵️Sailing weather! 5–15 kt — a great breeze to sail in.
  • 🛟Getting strong 16–23 kt — be careful out there.
  • 🌪️Too windy 24–30 kt — best to wait.
  • ☠️Stay ashore 31+ kt — too wild to sail.

Where’s this from?

This is the wind on the water at the Jamboree sailing venue. When the on-water anemometer at Górki Zachodnie (the National Sailing Centre) is reporting, that's the reading you see — the real breeze where the boats go out. If it's quiet, it falls back to the nearest working reading (the modelled wind at the Marina Błotnik point, then Gdańsk). The name under the picture always tells you which.

We keep the wind direction simple — one of eight main points: North, North-East, East, South-East, South, South-West, West and North-West.

The time shows when we last checked the station. Treat it as a live snapshot — and always look at the water too.

Wind and temperature from Holfuy #1441, Open-Meteo and IMGW-PIB. Always ask a leader and check the water before you sail.
