    Where’s this from?

    This is the modelled wind at the Marina Błotnik point on the Martwa Wisła, with the air temperature that comes with it. It’s a single-station start: we’ll add the on-water and coastal stations once the local winds — and where each station really sits — are better understood. The levels suit a stable training dinghy that carries a breeze well, so “sporty” runs higher than you’d set for a small single-hander.

    From experience: you might launch in 10 knots and cop 30+ in a gust — you wouldn’t have launched in that, but a reefed boat with weight aboard stays stable if you keep the sail flat, ease the gnav, and head in. Know your reef point before you go.

    We keep the wind direction simple — one of eight main points: North, North-East, East, South-East, South, South-West, West and North-West.
